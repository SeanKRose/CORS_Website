About the Society

What is operational research?

A field built on decisions

Operational research — also called operations research, analytics, or management science — uses mathematics, computing, engineering, and business thinking to improve how decisions get made. It shows up anywhere a hard choice needs to be solved systematically: transportation networks, hospital scheduling, energy grids, telecommunications, finance, and supply chains.

People working in OR tend to be equally comfortable with quantitative problem-solving and with the people side of implementing a solution, since most OR careers sit at the intersection of the two.

Why researchers and practitioners join CORS

CORS gives Canada's OR community — spanning university departments and industry teams alike — a shared home. Members build a national network of contacts, follow the latest research coming out of Canadian institutions, and get visibility for their own work through the Society's conferences, journal, and awards.
