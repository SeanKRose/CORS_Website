---
title: About CORS
---

The Canadian Operational Research Society (CORS) is the national professional society for operational research, analytics, management science, and operations management professionals in Canada.

CORS brings together academics, students, and industry practitioners who develop and apply analytical methods to improve decision-making.

Members benefit from:

- A national professional network
- Access to the INFOR journal
- Access to the CORS Bulletin
- Discounted conference registration
- Student chapters and special interest groups
- Professional development opportunities

Founded in 1957, CORS continues to support excellence in operational research across Canada.
