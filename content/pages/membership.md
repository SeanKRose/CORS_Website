Why join CORS?

As a member of CORS, you can:

- Build a network of Canadian OR professionals
- Learn about current research developments
- Receive reduced conference registration rates
- Access the INFOR journal
- Access the CORS Bulletin
- Join Special Interest Groups (SIGs)
- Participate in local sections and student chapters
- Receive job announcements through the members mailing list

Membership is open to academics, students, and practitioners working in operational research, analytics, and management science.
